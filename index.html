Appointment Text Messaging (SMS)

With your consent, Buckeye Physical Therapy may send SMS appointment reminders, confirmations, and scheduling messages. Opting in to texts is optional and is never required to receive care. You can opt in on our appointment request form (enter your phone number and check the optional consent box) or in person by giving verbal consent at your appointment. Message frequency varies (up to 4/month). Message & data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out, HELP for help. Mobile opt-in information is never shared or sold to third parties for marketing.
